# Light Hero progression: shared foundation and character identity

**Status:** Hybrid Light progression confirmed (D-042 through D-049), Forest Witch pilgrimage and ritual decisions (D-050 through D-062), and Goblin economy, secret tavern recapture, and two-scale theft progression (D-063 through D-069). Exact costs, item pools and unapproved mechanics remain open.
**Authority:** [Decision register](../decisions.md), particularly D-005, D-010, D-013, D-023, D-042, D-043, D-044, D-045, D-046, D-047, D-048 and D-049.
**Timing:** M2 should introduce a small credible shared progression loop; M3 can develop differentiated character identity. Do not interrupt current gameplay engineering to implement unapproved mechanics.

## Confirmed
- Every Light Hero participates in a **shared foundational progression layer of equipment and resources** (D-043). This provides a common acquisition and improvement language without requiring an XP bar or identical equipment loadouts.
- Each Light Hero also has a **distinct character-specific progression system**. Characters can advance through different activities and resources, and can use shared materials/equipment in different ways. Specific class-specific uses remain proposals, not approved kits.
- Light Heroes grow strong enough to threaten the Dark Lord and castle, while the Dark side hunts them and accelerates awakening.
- Light-controlled sanctuaries provide recovery **and character progression opportunities** (D-023); exactly how they contribute is open.
- **Hybrid resource acquisition (D-044):** Light Heroes find useful equipment and resources while exploring the kingdom, and earn larger rewards for completing meaningful world objectives. Individual characters may seek different activities and locations. Actual loot tables, missions, resource quantities and resource respawn policy are unapproved.
- **Hybrid upgrading (D-045):** Light Heroes can change gear, craft simple items, and perform minor improvements in the field. Significant upgrades require an appropriate sanctuary, workshop, or other specialized site. What counts as major/minor, exact facilities and resource costs remain open. Do not automatically require a return trip for routine preparation.
- **Hybrid resource ownership (D-046):** personal equipment, special ingredients and character-specific materials belong to the collecting Light Hero. Common resources earned through major objectives contribute to a **shared team pool**. Trade permissions, which rewards are classified as common, and protections against one player draining the pool are not yet approved.
- **Hybrid shared spending (D-047):** routine/small shared-pool purchases do **not** require a team-wide vote each time; costly/major investments require team authorization. **Prevent repeated minor purchases from draining the pool.**
- **Confirmed (D-048): hybrid spending allowances.** Every Light Hero earns a **baseline allowance from team successes**, plus **bonus spending rights for meaningful individual contributions**. Allowances can fund the player's smaller purchases or be **pledged voluntarily** to a major team purchase. They are **permissions to draw down actual shared resources, not additional currency**. Spending consumes the shared resource pool **exactly once** and reduces the relevant allocation. A contribution system must recognize scouting, support, defense and cooperation, not just last hits. Exact ratios, events, caps, resets and approval conditions remain unresolved.
- **Confirmed (D-049): voluntary pledges approve major investments.** A major investment proceeds when the pledges add up to its required allowance commitment **and** sufficient actual shared resources remain. **No second team vote.** An allowance already pledged must not simultaneously be spent on a minor purchase, and a completed purchase deducts the actual pool only once. How to reserve/release pledges, deal with changing pool balance, prevent spam proposals, and handle simultaneous purchases remains open for design and server validation.
- Character differences must preserve shared stealth, cover and detection foundations (D-013).

### Distinguish historical GDD from current canon
The GDD contains outdated or exploratory mechanics, including **permanent Light Hero elimination** that has been superseded by the body/ghost revival design (D-032 onward), and a fixed Dark victory condition tied to timer rather than the confirmed awakening/final battle. Old mechanic values and named abilities should be treated as **design candidates**, not silently hardcoded.

The four character identities and core loops are **already documented**. Do not ask the creator to choose a newly invented archetype before reading those sections. The next useful step is a **system-by-system reconciliation**, preserving each distinctive loop while resolving conflicts with our confirmed shared resource, sanctuary and stealth mechanics.

### Goblin Outlaw theft loop (CONFIRMED: D-068)

- **Frequent opportunistic theft:** the Goblin can steal valuables through quick, risky interactions with appropriate targets such as distracted or exposed individuals. These support recurring personal-gold income without requiring a full mission every time.
- **Larger planned heists:** the Goblin can identify a richer opportunity, learn guard patterns or defenses, infiltrate the location, take treasure, and escape. Bigger potential rewards must come with meaningfully different planning, exposure or escape demands rather than only a longer interaction timer.
- **Economy and stealth:** theft proceeds belong to the Goblin (D-063). Larger **team** world objectives still contribute common resources according to D-044/D-046. Both scales use legitimate vision, cover, sound and attention rules (D-012 through D-014), not unconditional invisibility or omniscient alarm triggers.
- **Caught theft: two timescales (D-069):** if a theft is **observed or legitimately detected**, local witnesses/guards react and pursue immediately. Repeated **detected** theft in the **same area** also raises its enduring suspicion and makes subsequent robbery attempts more challenging through heightened local vigilance. This cannot broadcast the Goblin's hidden coordinates, magically identify him after line of sight is broken, or automatically produce a global Dark-team alert. Apply D-012/D-013/D-014 perception, evidence and escape limits.
- **Open:** exact theft targets and heist locations, repeatability, rewards, which incidents count as detected, who responds, pursuit range/end, **area boundaries, suspicion thresholds and decay/reduction**, overlapping team objectives, and protection against farming easy targets. No numeric values are canon.

### Goblin Outlaw personal-gold economy (CONFIRMED: D-063)

- **Personal progression:** Gold stolen or otherwise earned through the Goblin's personal activities belongs to the Goblin, preserving the GDD's thief/economy playstyle and its hidden tavern as an upgrade destination. Do not treat this gold as an automatic team-wide currency.
- **Team contribution:** Major Light-team world objectives still contribute **common resources to the shared team pool** (D-044/D-046), including when the Goblin participates. This is not an exemption from the team's progression and pledge-funded spending rules (D-047/D-049).
- **Accounting boundary:** Personal gold and shared-pool resources are distinct authoritative holdings. A single reward cannot be issued twice under two ledgers without a separately approved split. Exact loot classification, monster gold drops, reward-splitting, resource conversion, trading, cost and inventory caps remain open.
- **Existing GDD tavern:** preserve the randomized **Secret Tavern** as the Goblin's established progression concept. **D-064 confirms Dark occupation temporarily disables its services but can be reversed**. Permanent destruction and automatic relocation were not selected. Exact upgrade catalog and tavern access remain open.
- **Goblin-led tavern recapture (D-064):** the Goblin can **infiltrate an occupied tavern, turn on brutal built-in defenses, and lead an attack with the tavern's hiding denizens** to expel occupying Dark forces. This is an intended gameplay possibility, **not an automatic victory**. Light allies can participate in reclamation, but the Goblin has the signature covert-defense-and-denizen approach. Details of infiltration, hidden defense controls, denizen rallying and AI, combat balance, denial rules and recapture state are not yet specified.
- **Concealed master mechanism (D-065):** The Goblin **sneaks to one hidden master control** inside the occupied tavern. Activating it **simultaneously brings its brutal defenses online and signals hidden denizens to attack with the Goblin**. No compulsory sequence of preparing several switches or progressive activation chain. This **initiates** a contested recapture fight rather than instantly restoring the shop. How occupiers guard or interfere with the mechanism, the physical interaction/feedback, timing, battle behavior, and consequences of a failed attack are not yet specified.
- **Dark guard counterplay (D-066):** Occupying Dark Heroes **may discover the hidden master control and station forces to guard it**, creating an infiltration/approach challenge. They **cannot destroy the control**. The disabled/sabotaged-mechanism branch was **not chosen**. Discovering the control does not automatically track the Goblin, and occupying the tavern does not guarantee discovery. Whether a defender can prevent activation by proximity, and which routes or deception tactics defeat a guarded position, need further approval. Protect the Goblin's eventual reclamation option against an unbreakable bottleneck.
- **Alternate approaches and deception (D-067):** the occupied tavern supports **multiple infiltration approaches, including concealed passages**, and the Goblin can **misdirect, distract or lure Dark defenders out of position** to reach the one concealed master mechanism. Both route choice and enemy manipulation are supported; neither alone is mandatory for every attempt. Dark occupiers can respond tactically without omniscient tracking, and mechanism activation is still contested rather than a free win. Exact geometry, entry discoverability, stealth tools, adversary AI and passage-blocking rules require later design. Avoid a single unavoidable campable doorway.
- **State boundary:** keep **available / occupied / reclaimed** as distinct conceptual states; while occupied, normal shop/upgrade services are unavailable. Do not silently delete the tavern, teleport it elsewhere, make every denizen invulnerable, or let occupation permanently remove the Goblin's progression.

### Forest Witch pilgrimage contract (CONFIRMED: D-050)
- **Team objective:** Every Light Hero can purify corrupted major landmarks. Team purification/conversion into sanctuary is governed by D-019 and D-022 and **is not Witch-exclusive**.
- **Purify first (D-052):** a shrine must be purified before the Witch can perform her **separate** one-time ley-line ritual. The Witch may purify it herself using the same general team action, or arrive after an ally has purified it. Her personal ritual **does not cleanse** a still-corrupted shrine.
- **Intuitive discovery (D-053/D-054):** the Witch senses **distant magical disturbances** without exact shrine locations. **Passive cues** emerge naturally as she explores; she may **focus her senses on demand** to sharpen direction/proximity information and interpret environmental clues. Neither mode provides exact pins or unseen enemy positions.
- **Focused sensing exposes magical activity (D-055):** deliberate focusing **emits a supernatural disturbance detectable by nearby Dark Scouts**. This presents an investigation risk, not an automatic sighting/continuous tracking channel. Passive cues do not emit this same deliberate disturbance. The previously proposed reduced-mobility/awareness tradeoff was **not selected**. Detectable range, Scout response, alert handling, duration and Witch-side readability remain open.
- **Scout-owner private clue (D-056):** if a nearby Scout detects this focused-sensing disturbance, **only that Scout's owning Dark Hero** gets a **time-stamped approximate-area report** of magical activity. It is **not** an identification, confirmed sighting, exact Witch coordinate, team-wide ping or live vision grant. The Scout can investigate the uncertainty through normal perception. The Dark team receives its standard shared alert **only after a genuinely confirmed visual sighting** (D-012). Reporting detail and presentation are open.
- **Witch progression:** The Witch can perform her special ley-line ritual at each eligible **shrine** to gain personal magical advancement. Each **unique shrine** grants this ritual's progression **at most once per match**.
- **Ritual rewards (D-051):** completing the one-time ritual grants **Arcane Power and a selection between shrine-themed abilities**. The shrine's identity shapes which powers are offered, while the player chooses how to specialize. The number of ability options, choice behavior and power effects are **not approved**.
- **Full reset on interruption (D-057):** if the Witch takes damage or her personal shrine rite is otherwise disrupted while channeling, the **ongoing attempt is cancelled with no retained progress**. She must restart it from zero at a purified, eligible shrine. A failed attempt does **not** mark that shrine's one-time reward as claimed, and successful completion remains the only award trigger. Full re-corruption removes eligibility until new purification, but does not undo an already claimed reward. Damage is confirmed as an interrupt, while additional disruption types, exact channel duration and other restrictions are open. Do **not** apply this rule to ghost resurrection or sanctuary ward restoration.
- **Interactive rite, not passive waiting (D-059):** advancing at a shrine requires the Witch to **actively manipulate ritual symbols or interact with ritual points** while exposed to possible interruption. The earlier choice of allowing movement inside a circle as the defining mechanic was **superseded by the creator's corrected C selection**. Do not add a mandated ring boundary, stationary-only channel, fixed minigame or free combat during the rite until those details are designed. Damage still cancels the whole attempt (D-057). Number/order of interactions, locomotion, targeting/controls, duration and accessibility remain open.
- **Shared ritual grammar, distinct shrine challenges (D-060):** the Witch learns a **reusable set of magical interactions** across shrines, with **each shrine introducing a distinctive challenge linked to its own theme and potential powers**. This is **not** an entirely different minigame per shrine, nor a generic identical sequence everywhere. The exact shared interaction verbs, shrine variations, number/order of steps, failure state, and UX remain open. Retain active play (D-059), full reset if interrupted (D-057), and the once-per-shrine reward (D-050).
- **Energy-direction interaction (D-061):** the reusable core of the ritual is **establishing and manipulating magical connections between points and redirecting currents through the shrine** until the required magical configuration is achieved. Shrine-specific challenges should modify how these currents behave or need to be arranged, rather than replacing this core with unrelated minigames. How the player physically targets/adjusts currents, whether flow is real-time or stepwise, how completion is judged, and the exact 3D/UI depiction remain unapproved.
- **Hybrid physical and magical controls (D-062):** the Witch **manipulates physical ritual objects** to alter paths or relationships in the shrine, **and directly guides or connects the resulting magical currents** between them. Those actions should operate as two facets of one ritual system, not separate unrelated puzzles. Different shrines may emphasize or combine them differently. Exact objects, controls, sequence and amount of player freedom remain open; no fixed gesture or number of interaction stages is approved.
- **Ritual exposure (D-058):** channeling a Witch shrine ritual causes **local supernatural visual/environmental effects and a magical disturbance that nearby Dark Scouts may detect and investigate**. It does **not** automatically identify her, give her current position, or issue a kingdom-wide alert to all Dark Heroes. Ritual magic is distinct from focused sensing (D-055/D-056), and whether a Scout detects and privately reports the former using the exact same reporting rules as the latter **is not yet decided**. Exact detection radius, cue strength, duration and feedback remain open.
- **World journey:** Repeated progress requires reaching **different** shrines. Exhausting all available shrine rewards requires visiting them across the kingdom. Returning to a shrine that was subsequently re-corrupted and purified **cannot reaward** its Witch-specific power.
- **Not yet approved:** Which map landmarks count as eligible shrines; how far focused-sensing disturbances travel and how Scouts detect them, plus private approximate report precision/lifetime; visual/audio/haptic cues, range and obstruction behavior; whether focusing also consumes a resource or has a cooldown (not implied by D-055); **physical apparatus manipulation controls, direct-current guidance/connection affordances, continuous versus stepwise flow, how these actions combine per shrine, shrine-specific variants and difficulty, movement/combat allowances, duration, interruption triggers beyond damage, and ritual disturbance detection radius and private-report behavior** (damage already cancels and fully resets the rite in D-057); ritual availability during an active siege while still Light-controlled; exact themed ability choices and Arcane Power values; and what happens if a shrine cannot be accessed. Full Dark re-corruption removes ritual eligibility until purified again. Interrupted, incomplete attempts never grant or consume the one-time reward.
- **Implementation future guardrail:** track a server-authoritative set of shrine identifiers whose **one-time Witch-specific progression reward was already claimed**, independent from current shrine faction ownership, Light sanctuary status, and general repeatable territorial objectives. Test capture/re-corruption loops cannot farm Witch progress. Avoid assuming all major landmarks are ley-line shrines.

## Open choices
1. **Pledge accounting and anti-abuse before coding:** decide withdrawal, reservation, purchase thresholds, expiry and handling competing major proposals. Voluntary pledges authorize major spending (D-049), with no separate vote. Exact contribution weights and allowance caps remain open.
2. **Character progression reconciliation:** preserve the original Goblin Outlaw, Alchemist, Exiled Knight and Forest Witch. The Witch's shrine progression is confirmed through D-062, and **the Goblin's personal-gold economy, reversible tavern occupation and uprising, and opportunistic theft plus larger heists are confirmed in D-063 through D-069**. Resolve concrete theft targets/alerts, tavern defense balance, and the remaining Alchemist recipes/Knight Valor interactions against the current shared economy and stealth rules without replacing their GDD concepts.
3. **Reward structure:** power ceiling, ability progression, unlock pacing, upgrade choices, and whether upgrades can be lost on death.
4. **Sanctuary role:** purchases, rituals, ability training, or character-specific interactions with purified landmarks?
5. **Match pacing:** ensure one Hero cannot dominate by repeated farming while others are occupied. Progress must remain practical in a multiplayer 4v4 match.
6. **Observability:** show the player what their next meaningful progression objective is without reducing each role to the same meter.

## Design standards for later implementation
- Shared progression should lower learning friction, **not erase character differences**.
- Every character must have at least one interesting decision about how to advance, rather than merely receiving passive stat increases.
- Advancement actions must create readable risks or map interactions for the opposing side.
- Resurrection and ghost play (D-032 onward) must not accidentally allow unbounded upgrade farming.
- The server must determine and validate earned progression. No client-declared XP, resource gains or unlocked abilities.
- **Do not treat example class systems, materials, currency numbers, or XP curves as approved.**
