# The Dark Lord — Dormancy, Presence and Awakening

**Status:** exploratory design proposal. **Creator-confirmed:** D-015, Dark Lord is the **dormant ruler and final castle objective/boss**. The active Dark Lord idea is intriguing to the creator but **NOT approved**.
**Date:** 2026-11-11.

## Core dramatic promise
The four playable Dark Heroes defend their sleeping master and struggle to protect the castle while four Light Heroes grow powerful enough to kill him. Even asleep, the Dark Lord's existence should give the kingdom a sense of escalating danger. The final castle assault should feel like the culmination of all eight players' decisions.

This document does not establish a time-to-wake rule, full boss phase, or autonomously roaming Dark Lord.

## Three levels of ambient presence worth considering

### Tier 1 — The kingdom responds (low complexity, potentially valuable)
Sleeping Dark Lord is *not* an active AI character. His influence appears through environment and strategic systems: encroaching corruption, changed monster routes, altered locations, ominous audiovisual events. Changes must be legible and mechanically meaningful, not random inconveniences.

**Value:** feeling that the world is alive, while keeping one central conflict. **Risk:** scripted atmosphere mistaken for actual decisions.

### Tier 2 — Dreams exert discrete influence (moderate complexity)
At specific game events, his dream state generates a clear, telegraphed effect: a location becomes haunted, minions receive a temporary directive, a ward awakens, or heroes encounter a predictable but unusual threat. Effects have clear triggers, counterplay and boundaries. These could be system-driven without requiring a full AI personality.

**Value:** evolving strategy and narrative buildup. **Risk:** arbitrary interventions can undermine either team's agency.

### Tier 3 — Autonomous ruler intelligence (high complexity)
An active strategic AI makes its own decisions behind the scenes, potentially directing the kingdom and reacting to hero behavior. It must be designed not to steal agency from the four Dark Heroes or expose hidden hero information unfairly. This is **not confirmed** and should not be added because it sounds impressive.

**Value:** unsettling sense of an intelligent sleeping power. **Risk:** a fifth quasi-player on Dark side, balance unpredictability, confusing attribution of actions, and additional network/AI scope.

## Questions the eventual system must answer
1. What causes awakening: a fixed clock, Dark Hero actions, hero proximity, kingdom corruption, or a hybrid?
2. Is awakening a Dark victory threshold, or the start of a final boss confrontation? Both at once could contradict the heroes' victory condition.
3. Does the boss actively fight during the final assault? What phases, telegraphs, and player counterplay?
4. Can Light Heroes sabotage awakening or temporarily weaken it through map objectives?
5. Do Dark Heroes cooperate or compete over actions that strengthen the master? How do their individual identities matter?
6. What changes in gameplay when the Dark Lord is 'stirring,' beyond ambience?
7. How do we communicate his influence without obscuring which Dark player caused a threat?

## Design guardrails
- Preserve **four Dark Hero player slots**. The Dark Lord is *not* a replacement fifth human player.
- Preserve fair stealth and perception rules (D-012, D-013 and D-014).
- Do not let a speculative autonomous ruler possess perfect knowledge of hidden Light Heroes.
- Signal emerging threats clearly enough that players can respond, while keeping the gothic dark-fairytale mood.
- Test any world influence against both teams' opportunities for meaningful choice.
- Do not spend M0/M1 engineering time on these concepts until selected, specified and prioritized.

## Recommendation
Keep **Tier 1** available as cheap visual/narrative scaffolding while evaluating **Tier 2** through later gameplay experiments. Hold **Tier 3** as a future research branch, not an implementation milestone.

**No tier is approved** by the creator yet. D-015 only confirms the dormant final-boss foundation.

## Next decision
Decide the **relationship between awakening and match victory** before designing the final battle. Otherwise the timer, hero pressure, and Dark Heroes' defensive strategy will conflict.
